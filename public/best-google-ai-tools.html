Pros
  • Powerful & free
  • Deep Google integration
  • Multimodal (text, image, voice)
Cons

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Google AI tool in 2026?
Gemini is Google’s best all-round AI tool. For research use NotebookLM, for developers Google AI Studio, for images ImageFX, and for video Google Flow.

Are Google AI tools free?
Most are free to start — Gemini, NotebookLM, AI Studio, ImageFX and Whisk all have free access. Advanced features (Gemini Advanced, Flow, Workspace AI) may need a paid plan.

What is NotebookLM?
NotebookLM is a free Google AI research assistant that answers questions from your own uploaded documents with citations, and can generate an audio “podcast” overview of your notes.

What is Gemini Flash?
Gemini Flash is Google’s fastest, most cost-efficient Gemini model, built for high-volume, low-latency tasks in apps and automations.
