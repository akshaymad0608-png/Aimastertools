Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI writing tool in 2026?
Jasper is the best overall for marketing teams thanks to brand voice and SEO features. For free, ChatGPT is the most versatile, while Claude produces the most natural long-form writing.

What is the best free AI writing tool?
ChatGPT and Claude both have strong free tiers for general writing, Copy.ai is best free for short marketing copy, and Grammarly is best free for editing.

Which AI writing tool is best for SEO?
Writesonic and Jasper are built with SEO workflows — keyword research and optimised article generation designed to help content rank.

Are AI writing tools safe to use for published content?
Yes, but always fact-check and edit. AI can invent details, so use it to draft faster and add your own expertise before publishing.
