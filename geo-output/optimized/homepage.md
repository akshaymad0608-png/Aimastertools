**What is AI Master Tools?**
A free, independently curated directory of 640+ AI tools across 49 categories. Each tool is hand-checked and listed with real pricing, honest pros and cons, ratings, and the best alternatives.

**Is AI Master Tools free to use?**
Yes — browsing, searching and comparing every tool is completely free, and 100+ of the listed tools have free plans.

**How are the tools chosen and ranked?**
Tools are hand-reviewed and ranked on capability, pricing and real-world usefulness — not on payment. There are no pay-to-rank or sponsored placements.

**How often is the directory updated?**
The directory is reviewed and refreshed every week, with new tools and updated pricing added regularly.
