Frequently asked questions

What is the best free AI tool in 2026?
ChatGPT is the most useful free AI tool overall. For specific jobs: Gemini (chat), Leonardo (images), Perplexity (research), Canva (design) and Codeium (coding) are the best free picks.

Are these AI tools completely free?
Each has a genuinely usable free tier — no payment to start. Some cap daily or monthly usage and reserve advanced features for paid plans.

What is the best free AI image generator?
Leonardo AI offers the most generous free image credits, with Ideogram, Adobe Firefly and Google Imagen also strong free options.

Do I need a credit card to use free AI tools?
Usually no — most of these only need a free account. Always check before signing up if that matters to you.
