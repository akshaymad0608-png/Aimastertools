Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI video generator in 2026?
Runway is the best all-round AI video generator. For photorealism, Google Veo and OpenAI Sora lead, and Synthesia is best for AI-avatar business videos.

What is the best free AI video generator?
Luma Dream Machine, Pika, Kling and Runway all offer usable free tiers to create AI video at no cost.

Which AI tool makes talking-avatar videos?
Synthesia and HeyGen are the leaders for AI presenters and avatars, ideal for training, explainer and marketing videos without filming.

Can I use AI-generated videos commercially?
Usually yes on paid plans, but rights vary by tool — check each provider’s license, especially for client work and ads.
