The Best Free AI Tools Online: 12 Picks for 2026

The best free AI tools in 2026 are ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Leonardo AI, Ideogram, Canva AI, CapCut, Suno AI, ElevenLabs and Windsurf. Each has a free plan you can start on without paying, but nearly all of them cap how much you can do, so the useful question is not whether a tool is free but how far free gets you. This page answers that for each one.

What are the best free AI tools?

Start with one of the three general assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) and add a specialist tool for the job you actually have. These are grouped by job, not ranked:

  • Chat and writing: ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude
  • Research with sources: Perplexity
  • Everyday assistant: Microsoft Copilot
  • Images: Leonardo AI, Ideogram
  • Design and social graphics: Canva AI
  • Video: CapCut
  • Music: Suno AI
  • Voice and narration: ElevenLabs
  • Coding: Windsurf

These are our picks, not a ranking by quality or popularity. Free plans change often, so check the vendor's page before you depend on one.

Free vs freemium: what “free” really means

“Free AI tool” covers several different deals, and the differences decide whether a tool will still work for you in a month:

Free or open source
Costs nothing to use. Open-source software is free to run, but you may still pay for the computer or hosting it runs on. None of the 12 picks here is this kind of tool.
Free plan (free tier)
A plan that stays free, with fewer features or lower usage limits than paid plans. Every pick here has one.
Freemium
The business model behind most of these tools: a free plan to get you started and paid plans for more. Freemium does not mean unlimited. All 12 picks are freemium.
Free trial
Full access for a limited period, after which you pay or lose access. A trial is not a free plan, and the tools here are included for their ongoing free plans.
Credits, tokens and quotas
A free allowance that is used up as you work and refills later. Leonardo AI (daily tokens), Suno AI (daily credits), Ideogram (weekly credits), ElevenLabs (monthly credits), Canva (a monthly AI allowance) and Windsurf (a usage quota) work this way. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot instead apply usage limits that vary by feature and demand.
Personal versus commercial use
Free does not always mean free for business. Suno and ElevenLabs both say their free plans are for non-commercial use.

Which free AI tool should you start with?

  • If you need a general assistant for writing and everyday questions: start with ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude. All three have a free plan with usage limits. Try one for a week on real tasks before deciding.
  • If you need research where you want to see the sources: start with Perplexity. It is built around searching the web, and standard searches are available on the free plan.
  • If you need images: start with Leonardo AI, Ideogram. Both work on an allowance (daily tokens at Leonardo, weekly credits at Ideogram), so the better fit is the one whose allowance matches how many images you need.
  • If you need graphics, presentations or social posts: start with Canva AI. The editor and templates matter more than the AI features here, and the free AI allowance is limited each month.
  • If you need short social videos: start with CapCut. The free Standard plan covers essential editing; check which AI features need Pro before planning around one.
  • If you need music or voice-over: start with Suno AI, ElevenLabs. Both free plans are for non-commercial use, so they suit experiments, not client or commercial work.
  • If you need help writing code: start with Windsurf. Unlimited Tab completions and inline edits on the free plan make it a low-risk place to start.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best free AI tools?

Our 12 picks are ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Leonardo AI, Ideogram, Canva AI, CapCut, Suno AI, ElevenLabs and Windsurf. They are grouped by job rather than ranked, and each has a free plan with limits.

Which AI tools are completely free?

None of the 12 picks on this page is unlimited. Each has a free plan with usage limits or an allowance of credits, tokens or quota, and some keep commercial use or advanced features for paid plans. Fully free tools exist, and our free AI tools pages list them apart from tools that only have a free tier.

Is ChatGPT free to use?

Yes. ChatGPT has a Free plan that includes chat, web search, file uploads and image creation. Features such as uploads, image creation, voice and data analysis have separate usage limits that are stricter than on paid plans.

What is the best free AI image generator?

Leonardo AI (a daily token allowance), Ideogram (a weekly credit allowance with a slower free queue) and Canva (a limited monthly allowance of AI uses) all have free plans for images. Which suits you depends on the look you want and how many images you need.

Which free AI tool is best for coding?

Windsurf is our coding pick. Its Free plan includes unlimited Tab completions and inline edits, plus a light quota for agent coding, according to its pricing page.

Can I use free AI output commercially?

Not always. Suno says songs made on its Free plan are for non-commercial use, and ElevenLabs says its free plan cannot be used for any commercial purpose. Check each tool's terms before you publish anything for business use.

Do I need a credit card to use free AI tools?

We have not verified sign-up requirements for each tool, and they can change. Free access requires an Ideogram account; current sign-up options and free-credit availability can vary by login method. Check the sign-up page before you start.
