Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI logo maker in 2026?
Looka is the best overall for a professional logo and brand kit. Canva is the best free option, and Brandmark is best for a complete brand identity.

What is the best free AI logo maker?
Canva’s logo maker is the best free option to design and download a logo, with Ideogram and Adobe Express also strong free choices.

Can I get vector (SVG) files from an AI logo maker?
Yes — Recraft outputs true vectors, and paid plans on Looka, Brandmark and others include SVG/EPS files for print and scaling.

Are AI-generated logos unique?
They’re original designs, but templates can look similar. For a distinctive mark, customise the result and check it isn’t close to an existing brand before using it.
