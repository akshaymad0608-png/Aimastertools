Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI chatbot in 2026?
ChatGPT is the best all-round chatbot. Claude is best for writing and coding, Gemini is the best free option, and Perplexity is best for research with cited sources.

What is the best free ChatGPT alternative?
Google Gemini is the strongest free alternative, with Claude, Microsoft Copilot and DeepSeek also offering capable free tiers.

Which AI chatbot is best for up-to-date information?
Perplexity and Google Gemini are best for current facts — Perplexity cites its web sources, and both can search in real time.

Which AI chatbot is most private?
Self-hosted or open models like DeepSeek give the most control. On mainstream chatbots, check each provider’s data settings and turn off training on your chats where possible.
